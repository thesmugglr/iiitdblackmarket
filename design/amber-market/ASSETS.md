# Asset provenance

The twelve product photos are from [Unsplash](https://unsplash.com/license), downloaded as optimized WebP files for a stable, self-contained preview. Exact original image URLs and local filenames are in [public/asset-sources.json](public/asset-sources.json). They are illustrative photography, not pictures of actual campus inventory. No remote images or fonts are requested while browsing.

DM Sans and Fraunces are self-hosted from the [Google Fonts repository](https://github.com/google/fonts). Their SIL Open Font Licenses are retained in `public/fonts/OFL-DM-Sans.txt` and `public/fonts/OFL-Fraunces.txt`. Icons come from Lucide (ISC license, installed dependency); decorative circles, the wordmark, and grain are CSS/SVG authored for this concept.

All listing descriptions, seller names, class years, badges, ratings, and quoted student stories are sample content. The page and detail views identify the preview status. No institute endorsement or completed student trades are claimed.
